HANDOFF: CVEngine driver monitoring (Rivian hackathon 2026)
Written for: a new Claude chat that continues this CVEngine work with no memory of the previous one.
Status as of 2026-11-07.

======================================================================
1. GOAL AND HARD REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================
Build a LIVE, causal (no lookahead, frame-by-frame) in-cabin driver-monitoring pipeline in
RivianHackatonEngine/CVEngine/. It runs on a Mac (Python 3.9, MPS GPU). For every analyzed frame it outputs
(timestamp, one byte) for the AlertEngine and GameEngine.

The user stressed that it must be live ("I HAVE TO BE LIVE"), that hands-off detection must be accurate, and that
ANY head movement off the road should trigger the output. It must work in both dark and light cabins.
Allowed head movement (a mirror check, a look into a turn) is NOT filtered in CV: the downstream engines own all
duration thresholds.

======================================================================
2. OUTPUT INTERFACE (agreed, documented)
======================================================================
One record per analyzed frame: timestamp (seconds, video time) and flags (one byte).

  Value        Meaning                                            Status
  0x80 (128)   fell asleep                                        NOT implemented
  0x40 (64)    looked off the road (head turned, down or up)      implemented, being validated
  0x20 (32)    hands off the steering wheel                       implemented
  0x10 (16)    phone distraction                                  NOT wired live
  0x0F (1-15)  reserved                                           always 0

The record is now THREE fields: timestamp,flags,occupants (occupants = people in the car, front and rear, from occupancy.py;
informational only, a lower bound). Stream example: 12.346,96,3. See the interface docs, section 3.1.

Bits add up: 96 = 0x40 + 0x20. A higher bit means a higher priority. Each bit is a STATE, not an event.
A 0 never proves absence: when nothing can be observed, the bit is 0.

Docs: AlertEngine/CV_ENGINE_INTERFACE.md and GameEngine/CV_ENGINE_INTERFACE.md (both untracked, uncommitted).
They cover the byte layout, the record format, the on_frame rules (validate, mask &0xF0, edge-detect each bit,
order 1->0 before 0->1), per-engine implications and acceptance tests.

NOT implemented: SafetySystem.on_frame in main.py (outside the CV folder). It is listed in requested_changes.md.
Bit-to-action mapping: 0x80 EYES_CLOSED, 0x40 EYES_OFF_ROAD, 0x20 HANDS_OFF_WHEEL, 0x10 PHONE_USE.

======================================================================
3. HOW TO RUN
======================================================================
From RivianHackatonEngine/CVEngine, with the same python3 and venv as before (the OLD venv, which has MediaPipe):

  python3 live.py videos/<name>.mp4 --stride 4 --stream      # prints "timestamp,byte" lines to stdout

Options:
  --save out.mp4      annotated video
  --frames x.csv      detail CSV
  --flags x.csv       byte CSV (frame,time_s,flags,hands_off,eyes_off_road)
  --output x.csv      simple 0/1 hands-off CSV (1 = no hands on wheel)
  --realtime          pace a video file at its own fps
  --head face|pose    default face
  --method hybrid|pose|mediapipe    default hybrid
  --stream            records to stdout, every other message to stderr
Camera: a camera number instead of a file, e.g. "0" (UNTESTED).

Speed: about 7-8 fps analyzed, 130-140 ms per frame, the same at stride 2 and stride 4. At 30 fps source, stride 4
is borderline (133 ms between frames vs 137 ms mean processing, p95 160 ms). Stride 5 is safer.

4. ARCHITECTURE (files in CVEngine/)
======================================================================

                   stream and CSVs and draws the overlay.

                   hands-on-wheel logic. Hybrid = MediaPipe hands where found, pose "near" kept, pose "away"
                   with no MediaPipe hand -> unknown. Hand states near/away/unknown; frame label near/off/unknown.

eyes_off_road.py   head-pose detector (see section 5).

                   gaze score. ~9 ms per call.

======================================================================
5. HEAD / EYES-OFF-ROAD DESIGN (current)
======================================================================
Source: face-model angles when a face is found (81-100% of frames), otherwise pose-keypoint proxies
(yaw = (nose_x - eye_mid_x) / eye_distance).
Signs: yaw negative = turned toward the image LEFT; pitch positive = head down. "left"/"right" are image sides.

Rule:
  turned  when |yaw - baseline| >= 20 deg   (hysteresis: leaves below 14)
  down    when pitch - baseline >= 12 deg   (exit 8)
  up      when baseline - pitch >= 15 deg   (exit 10)
  Pitch is only evaluated when the head is not turned.
  No face and no usable keypoints -> state "unknown", bit 0.

Why a baseline: the camera sees the driver from the side, so "looking at the road" is not 0 deg. Over 50 clips the
forward yaw ranged -35 to +51 deg, mostly near +8 or near +33.

Calibration, in three parts:
  1. POSITION PRIOR: the forward angle is predicted from the head position in the frame (nose x, nose y, eye
     distance); linear fit on 42 of 50 clips (FWD_YAW_COEF, FWD_PITCH_COEF). Leave-one-clip-out error: 7.7 deg yaw
     (p90 15.8), 5.4 deg pitch (p90 10.4).
  2. WARM-UP: the first 4 s (and at least 15 observations) only collect data. State "unknown / calibrating",
     bit stays 0.
  3. LEARNING: baseline = most frequent pose over the last ~2 minutes (weighted histogram mode). Each sample is
     weighted by a Gaussian around the prior (sigma 20 deg yaw, 10 deg pitch). The prior counts as 4 s of
     observations (PRIOR_SECONDS). The baseline keeps following the driver.

Measured calibration quality (baseline error vs each clip's full-video mode, 42 clips with a clear forward pose):
  yaw 7.0 deg at 8 s, 5.1 at 15 s, 2.9 at 29 s. The earlier fixed-prior median gave 10.4, 8.5 and 5.7.
  This is an in-sample proxy, not hand-labelled truth.

Evidence kept for sleep detection: blink (eye closure) and gaze-down scores are recorded per frame in the detail CSV
and are not used by the off-road rule.

7. KNOWN PROBLEMS / NEXT IMPROVEMENTS
======================================================================
 1. HEAD-DOWN DETECTION IS WEAK. In 011124 at 8.07 s (the user's screenshot) the look-down was not flagged: pitch
    was only +2-4 deg above the baseline, and the head was flagged "turned left" at 7.6-8.1 s instead. The pose
    proxy pitch is weaker still. Next: look at that frame, check the pitch baseline, consider gaze-down or a
    nose-to-chin cue. The pitch clamp pinned at 6 deg in 020303.
 2. START-OF-DRIVE WEAKNESS. A driver not looking at the road for most of the first ~15 s gets a wrong baseline
    (seen in 022115: position prior said 16 deg, true forward ~33 deg). Ideas: a better prior; a speed signal from
    the other engines (calibrate only while the car is moving); a longer or smarter calibration.
 3. NO GROUND-TRUTH LABELS. Priors, thresholds and calibration quality are in-sample (about 50 clips, one cabin
    camera). Needs hand-labelled clips. "Up" has hardly any validation. For another car or camera the coefficients
    must be refit.
 4. CHECK THE FLAGGED STRETCHES VISUALLY for the busy videos (010122, 010945, 022115, 011221) and whether the
    false-alarm rate is acceptable.
 5. POSE FALLBACK: its baseline uses different units and its own constants; rarely used (1-11% of frames) but the
    least calibrated.
 6. A head turned so far that nose and eyes vanish reads as "unknown" (bit 0): known limitation.
 7. SLEEP DETECTION (0x80) NOT BUILT. The user wants eye-closed duration, then classification as sleep vs loss of
    consciousness. The blink score is already recorded. Needs duration logic and thresholds.
 8. PHONE BIT (0x10) not wired into the live output.
 9. SPEED: ~7 fps. For safety on a 30 fps camera use stride 5, or speed up by threading pose and MediaPipe or by
    MediaPipe tracking mode. A camera feed that drops stale frames is untested.
10. HANDS-OFF ACCURACY was only visually checked on a few videos; false-alert rates on all 50 are unknown.
11. INTEGRATION: main.py needs SafetySystem.on_frame (requested_changes.md). Open points in the interface docs:
    gap handling (2 s proposal), smoothing, phone granularity, availability.
12. HOUSEKEEPING: update requirements.txt (mediapipe==0.10.21, protobuf==4.25.9, attrs==26.1.0). Partial
    out_live/eyes2_..010603 files could be deleted (asked, no answer).
